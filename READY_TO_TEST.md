## 📸 Recording Tips

### ✅ DO:
- Side angle (90° to shooter)
- Good lighting
- Clear background
- Full body in frame
- Record 5-10 seconds

### ❌ DON'T:
- Front angle
- Too dark
- Cluttered background
- Too short (<3 sec)

## 🐛 Quick Troubleshooting

### App won't load?
```bash
# Restart with cleared cache
npm start -- --clear
```

### Phone can't connect?
- Check both on **same WiFi**
- Disable **VPN**
- Try manual URL entry

### Camera not working?
- Grant **camera permissions** in phone settings
- Restart Expo Go app
